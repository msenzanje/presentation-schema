Redesign the UI of my trading app’s registration page so it looks elegant, modern, and professional. Keep all existing functionality, form fields, validation logic, and routing intact. Only change the visual design, layout, and styling.
Color palette
Background: warm off-white (around 
#F7F4EC), used for the page and form surfaces, with pure white only for input fields and cards to create subtle depth
Primary: deep evergreen (around 
#0F3D2E), used for the primary button, headings, the brand panel, and focus states
Accent: muted antique gold (around 
#C9A24B), used sparingly for highlights such as thin dividers, link hover states, small icons, the active step indicator, and a hairline border on key elements. Gold should never dominate; it should feel like a finishing touch.
Text: dark evergreen-tinted charcoal (around 
#1C2B25) for body text, and a softer green-gray (around 
#5F6F67) for helper text and placeholders
Feedback colors: a muted deep red for errors and the evergreen for success, both with sufficient contrast
Ensure all text meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 minimum)
Layout
Use a two-column split layout on desktop: a left brand panel in dark evergreen with the logo, a short tagline about trading with confidence, and 2 to 3 concise trust points (e.g., “Bank-grade security”, “Regulated and transparent”, “Real-time market data”), each with a thin gold line icon. Place the registration form on the right on the off-white background.
On mobile, collapse to a single column with a compact evergreen header above the form
Center the form in a card with a max width of about 440px, generous padding, and soft rounded corners (12 to 16px)
Use a subtle, low-opacity pattern or gradient on the evergreen panel (such as faint chart lines or a soft radial glow) for depth without clutter
Typography
Pair an elegant serif for headings (e.g., Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond) with a clean sans-serif for body and inputs (e.g., Inter)
Clear hierarchy: a large heading (“Create your account”), a short supportive subheading, then labels, inputs, and helper text
Use comfortable line height and consistent spacing on an 8px grid
Form components
Inputs: white background, 1px soft green-gray border, 10 to 12px radius, 48px height, visible labels above fields (not placeholder-only). On focus, show an evergreen border with a soft gold-tinted glow ring.
Password field: show/hide toggle and a slim strength meter that moves from muted red to gold to evergreen
Primary button: full-width, evergreen background, off-white text, subtle hover lift, and a slight gold underline or glow on hover. Include clear disabled and loading states.
Secondary options (e.g., “Sign up with Google/Apple”): outlined buttons with evergreen border and text
Checkbox for terms and conditions styled with an evergreen check; links in evergreen with a gold underline on hover
A “Already have an account? Log in” line at the bottom, with the link in evergreen
Inline validation messages that appear below each field, with small icons and gentle transitions
Polish and interaction
Soft, layered shadows (never harsh black); thin 1px borders instead of heavy outlines
Smooth 150 to 250ms transitions on hover, focus, and validation changes
Visible keyboard focus states and proper aria labels for accessibility
A small security note near the submit button (lock icon, “Your data is encrypted and protected”) in muted text
Avoid clutter, neon colors, and gradients that clash with the palette. The overall feel should be calm, trustworthy, and premium, like a private bank rather than a flashy crypto app.
